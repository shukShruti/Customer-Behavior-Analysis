
# Sales Performance PPT Template

## Overview
This repository contains a **Sales Performance PowerPoint Template** designed for business reviews, academic projects, internships, and corporate presentations. The template provides a structured and professional layout to present sales data, trends, KPIs, and strategic insights clearly.

## Purpose
The template helps users:
- Present sales performance in a clear and visual manner
- Analyze revenue trends and growth
- Highlight product, regional, and customer performance
- Summarize challenges and propose actionable recommendations

## Slides Included
1. **Title Slide**
   - Report title
   - Company name
   - Time period (Monthly / Quarterly / Annual)

2. **Agenda**
   - Overview of topics covered in the presentation

3. **Sales Overview**
   - Total sales revenue
   - Units sold
   - Overall growth rate
   - Comparison with previous period

4. **Revenue Trend Analysis**
   - Month-wise or quarter-wise revenue
   - Growth and decline patterns
   - Seasonal impact on sales

5. **Product Performance**
   - Top-performing products
   - Low-performing products
   - Category-wise sales contribution

6. **Regional Performance**
   - Top revenue-generating regions
   - Region-wise growth comparison
   - Underperforming regions

7. **Customer Insights**
   - New vs returning customers
   - Purchase frequency
   - High-value customer segments

8. **Key Sales KPIs**
   - Average Order Value (AOV)
   - Conversion rate
   - Customer retention rate
   - Sales target achievement

9. **Challenges & Risks**
   - Market competition
   - Seasonal sales fluctuations
   - Pricing or discount-related issues

10. **Recommendations**
    - Focus on high-performing products
    - Targeted marketing strategies
    - Customer retention programs
    - Pricing and discount optimization

11. **Conclusion**
    - Overall sales performance summary
    - Key takeaways
    - Next steps

## How to Use
1. Download the PPT template.
2. Open it in Microsoft PowerPoint or Google Slides.
3. Replace placeholder text with your actual sales data.
4. Insert charts (bar, line, pie) where required.
5. Customize colors, fonts, and themes as per your brand or project.
